Privacy Policy for enockfabre22-bot

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Welcome to enockfabre22-bot ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact the developer via the official repository.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise when you contact us. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services. We collect the following data through third-party APIs:

  • TikTok Account Data: When you use the TikTok Login Kit, we request the user.info.basic scope to display your basic profile information (such as username) locally in your dashboard.
  • Media Content: We process the video files (MP4) you choose to upload via the Content Posting API (using video.publish and video.upload scopes).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our Services for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process: If you choose to link your account with us to a third-party account (such as your TikTok account), we use the information you allowed us to collect from those third parties to facilitate the publishing process.
  • To provide the requested service: Your OAuth access tokens are securely utilized strictly to authorize the upload and publishing of your generated animations to your own social media profile.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Security

We keep your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy notice unless otherwise required by law. We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures (including secure token storage) designed to protect the security of any personal information we process.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we collect from you, change that information, or delete it in some circumstances. You may also revoke API access at any time directly from your TikTok account's security settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
