Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Football Video Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information processed through our application.

1. Information We Access

When you authenticate via TikTok Login Kit, we request access only to the scopes authorized by you: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and video publishing permissions (video.upload and video.publish).

2. How We Use Your Data

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in a private environment configuration and are used exclusively to upload and publish creator-approved football simulation videos to your TikTok account.

3. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data or tokens with third parties. You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account security settings.
